## Notes

- `LS1` is the off-board speaker, deliberately unpriced — `J5` is the connector on the board.
- All five 2.54 mm headers are cut from one 1x40 strip (20 pins of 40), so the strip is counted once.
- Almost everything here is a JLCPCB *Extended* part, which carries a $3 setup fee each if you
  use their assembly service; for a one-off, hand assembly is cheaper than the setup fees.

## If the STSPIN220 is out of stock

It is the scarcest part in the design. The closest replacement is the **DRV8834**
(`C400392`, VQFN-24, or `C456273`, HTSSOP-24, ~$2.40). Its 2.5–10.8 V range still covers a
3x AA pack for its useful life, but it is not a drop-in: different footprint, and KiCad 9
ships no DRV8834 symbol, so the motors sheet would need a custom symbol and footprint.
